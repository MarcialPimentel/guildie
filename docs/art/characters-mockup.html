Step 1 · audit

What the game shows today (0.9.0)

I ran the game locally the way CI does (MariaDB, the PHP API with fast pace, the realtime room server, desktop Chromium with software WebGL, ?view=3d&debug=1) and played as a Smith with three other players in the Smiths' quarter.

Works well

  • The guild hats are visible from the default camera: red cap, blue cone and straw hat tell you the guild.
  • Wobble, the stop bounce, the "Ciao!" and "Challenge!" bubbles and the white slap-off flag all work.
  • Townsfolk keep a quiet quarter alive; echoes at 42% opacity are clear.
  • Cost is fine today: 97 draw calls in a quarter with 4 players and 5 townsfolk.

Gaps

  • Customers: still capsules, all one shape; only the hat color changes (by order kind). Donna Vittoria looks the same as Captain Vos.
  • Crowding: arrivals stack at the Steps, and labels overlap each other.
  • Same guild, same look: two Smiths differ only by a pastel body color.
  • The glove is about 20 pixels at the quarter camera, so the bubble does the work.
  • Empty posts: nobody stands at the guild hall door (the trainer's place).
  • Budget: a blob is 4 draw calls and about 930 triangles, over the 600 in art-direction §9.
Step 2 · proposal

Who lives in the city

One character kit for everyone: the same blob, dressed from a table of parts (dress, hat, one prop in the hand, an optional sash). No skeletons and no model files. A new character is a few lines of data, which is what #51's hired hands will need.

How they move

Same spring wobble as 0.9.0, extended with a small set of moves every character shares. Everything is squash, lean and hand positions; nothing needs bones. With reduced motion, each move becomes a still pose held for the same time, with no bounce or squash.

  • One bubble per character, at most three on screen; the newest replaces the oldest. Lines are cut to about 60 characters; the full text is in the panel.
  • Labels never stack: name tags that would overlap are nudged up in a small column; your own merchant's tag stays hidden unless you hover over it.
  • Arrivals spread out: people entering a quarter step off the Steps onto a fan of eight spots instead of one.

Decisions I need from you

  1. Give each named customer their own look (9 looks, from the table above). Recommended. The alternative is one generic customer that only changes hat color.
  2. Customers speak and take the goods (scenes 1 and 2). Recommended.
  3. Buyers walk to your stall for real sales (scene 3), at most 3 per refresh. Recommended.
  4. Add bow, cheer and shrug to wave and slap, for everyone in the quarters. Recommended. Idle behaviors come with it.
  5. Rank sashes now (#30): no sash, sea-glass, blue, then gold with a medallion. Recommended.
  6. Let players choose their look: six body colors and three hats per guild, free, saved on the server (migration 008). Recommended now. The alternative is to wait and later tie hats to ranks or the Regatta. Unlocking hats would be a gameplay decision.
  7. The trainer and the herald get parts: an elder at each guild hall door, and the herald's trumpet for Duke's orders. Recommended.
  8. Ship it as 0.10.0 "the people of Portovela", one PR with the kit, the fixes from the audit (crowding, labels, budget) and whatever you approve above. Recommended.

Not for this session

  • In the quarter, the three new players had no bottega on the piazza (all twelve said "To let") even though they were standing there. Stalls are filled from recent activity on the server, so a brand-new crafter has no stall yet. That's a quarter rule, not a character question.
