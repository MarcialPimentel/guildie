# Guildie Playtest 1: the Market Prototype

Thanks for testing! Guildie is a crafting and trading game. You join one guild, make goods, and sell them to the city and to each other. This first test is about one question: **is making a good trade fun?**

**Play at:** https://marz.gg/games/guildie/ (works on your phone's browser)

This is a prototype: plain screens, no art yet, and numbers that will change. Your progress may be reset between tests.

## How to play

1. **Create an account** (any name, a simple password) and **choose a guild**:
   - **Blacksmith:** copper and iron. Makes Brass Fittings, Lanterns, Iron Ingots, Swords.
   - **Alchemist:** herbs. Makes Healing Draughts, Smelting Flux, Tanning Solution, Steam Tonic.
   - **Leatherworker:** hides. Makes Leather Grips, Satchels, Cured Leather, Jerkins.
   Try to spread out so all three guilds have players.
2. **Workshop:** your gathering station fills up over time (about an hour to fill). Tap **Collect**. When it's full, it stops producing, so check in now and then.
3. **Craft** (further down the Workshop): pick a recipe, choose how many, tap **Craft**. You have **two crafting slots**. Collect finished crafts at the top of the Workshop.
4. **Market:**
   - **Browse** to see prices. Tap an item to buy it or see who's been selling.
   - **Sell** to list your goods. A small deposit and a 5% cut come out of the money when something sells, so you can list even with 0 gold.
   - **Quick sale** prices sell to traders within a few minutes, but for less.
   - **Sell to the guild** for instant gold with no listing. It pays the least, and less the more of one item you sell, so use it when you need gold now.
5. **Blue ingredients** come from other guilds. The best goods (swords, tonics, jerkins) need them, so you'll have to buy from other players, or from the Guild Suppliers at a premium.
6. **City:** see every crafter with their gold and crafts made (tap **Gold** or **Crafts** to sort). The richest crafter is **Il Factotum della Città**. Tap someone to see what they're selling, or **Propose a trade**: pick what you give and what you want back. What you offer is held until they accept or decline (or a day passes). Offers waiting for you show as **City (1)**; answer them in **City → Trades**.
7. **Feedback:** tap **Feedback** at the top of the screen anytime to send Marz a note.
8. **Orders (new in 0.5.0):** the Workshop has an order board with three customers. Each wants specific items before a deadline and pays more than the market, sometimes with a rare material too. **Fill** an order when you have the items, or **Turn it down** for a new customer (you can only turn one down every so often). After about 30 crafts, some orders ask for another guild's goods as well.
9. **The ticker (new in 0.5.0):** on the Market, every price shows how it compares with usual. **Green ▲** means above usual (a good time to sell); **red ▼** means below (a good time to buy). The **City news** at the top says why: the day of the Guild Week (Friday is Tournament Day!) and events such as a mine collapse.
10. **Little surprises:** collecting can turn up a rich vein, a bumper crop or a prize pelt, and crafts sometimes come out with a bonus item.
11. **While you were away:** come back after a while and the game shows what happened, with one tap to collect everything.

## New in 0.12.0: the long road to 100

- **Every guild now has 16 recipes, all the way to skill 100.** Open the guild trainer (the guild hall door in 3D, or the Recipes list in the Bottega tab) to see what you can learn next, what each recipe needs and which other guild's goods it uses.
- **New raws:** at skill 40 your station starts gathering a third raw (Coal, Frostcap or Eelskin), and at 65 a fourth (Silver Ore, Starbloom or Fine Fur).
- **New rare recipes** for Expert and Master can turn up on orders.
- **Bigger orders as you rank up:** higher ranks get orders sized for their goods.
- **The last bottega upgrade** (which opens Master) now needs Gears, Machine Oil and Drive Belts.
- **Questions for this playtest:** Is there always a recipe worth learning next? Do you have a goal (a recipe or a good) you're saving up for? Which other guild's goods did you need first, and could you get them?

## New in 0.11.0: the Palazzo and your bottega

- **Your bottega starts as a rundown shack.** At skill 25 your skill stops and crafts give no XP. Open your bottega (the 3D bottega door, or the Bottega tab) to see the upgrade that opens Journeyman, what it needs and what you have. Gather or buy the goods, build it, wait for the builders, then open it: you become a Journeyman and the shack becomes a repaired workshop.
- **Up the Steps → the Palazzo:** the square where all three guilds meet. Click the Wanted board to post a request or fill someone else's; the Hall of honor for the city's best; and the notary, the crier and the steward for a rumor and one favor each per day. On a phone: City → Palazzo.
- **Questions for this playtest:** Did the shack make you want to rebuild? Was the wall at 25 clear, and did building feel like an event? Did you use a rumor to trade? Did you post or fill a Wanted request?

## Things to know

- **The city shops every 5 minutes.** Townsfolk, adventurers and guild workshops buy goods; prices go up when things are scarce and down when the market is flooded.
- **Everything happens on the server**, so you can close the game anytime. Your station and crafts keep going.
- The game is sped up for testing: crafts take seconds to minutes, and stations fill in about an hour.
