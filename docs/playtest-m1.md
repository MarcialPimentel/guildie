# Guildie Playtest 1: the Market Prototype

Thanks for testing! Guildie is a crafting and trading game. You join one guild, make goods, and sell them to the city and to each other. This first test is about one question: **is making a good trade fun?**

**Play at:** https://marz.gg/games/guildie/ (works on your phone's browser)

This is a prototype: plain screens, no art yet, and numbers that will change. Your progress may be reset between tests.

## How to play

1. **Create an account** (any name, a simple password) and **choose a guild**:
   - **Blacksmith:** copper and iron. Makes Brass Fittings, Lanterns, Iron Ingots, Swords.
   - **Alchemist:** herbs. Makes Healing Draughts, Smelting Flux, Tanning Solution, Steam Tonic.
   - **Leatherworker:** hides. Makes Leather Grips, Satchels, Cured Leather, Jerkins.
   Try to spread out so all three guilds have players.
2. **Workshop:** your gathering station fills up over time (about an hour to fill). Tap **Collect**. When it's full, it stops producing, so check in now and then.
3. **Craft:** pick a recipe, choose how many, tap **Craft**. You have **two crafting slots**. Come back to the Workshop to collect finished crafts.
4. **Market:**
   - **Browse** to see prices. Tap an item to buy it or see who's been selling.
   - **Sell** to list your goods. You pay a small deposit; the Exchange takes 5% when something sells.
   - **Quick sale** prices sell to traders within a few minutes, but for less.
5. **Blue ingredients** come from other guilds. The best goods (swords, tonics, jerkins) need them, so you'll have to buy from other players, or from the Guild Suppliers at a premium.

## Things to know

- **The city shops every 5 minutes.** Townsfolk, adventurers and guild workshops buy goods; prices go up when things are scarce and down when the market is flooded.
- **Everything happens on the server**, so you can close the game anytime. Your station and crafts keep going.
- The game is sped up for testing: crafts take seconds to minutes, and stations fill in about an hour.
