# Guildie — Changelog

_Current as of 0.19.0 (2026-10-30). One entry per build, newest first, from the merged pull requests. The version is in [`VERSION`](VERSION); the live build shows in the game's footer._

## 0.19.0 — 2026-10-30
- People walk with stepping feet and a little waddle ([#78](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/78)).
- Anyone can be slapped: townsfolk, customers, officials, vendors and trainers get knocked back and say something. Only you see it, and nothing in the game changes. Slapping works on the quay too.

## 0.18.1 — 2026-10-30
- Fix: hovering the quay picks the place you're pointing at (the storehouse no longer steals the bench's hover) ([#84](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/84)).

## 0.18.0 — 2026-10-30
- Crafted goods come out Common, Fine or Masterwork. The easier a recipe is for you, the better you make it: learn with new recipes, or excel with old ones ([#83](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/83)).
- Fine goods are worth 1.5x and Masterwork 2.5x; you pick which quality to sell. Recipes, orders and upgrades take any quality, plainest first.

## 0.17.2 — 2026-10-30
- Faster deploys: a merge goes live in about 2 minutes instead of 8, because the tests already ran on the pull request ([#81](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/81)).
- Design only, no game change: the second profession and changing guild were designed ([#82](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/82), GDD 5.7–5.8).

## 0.17.1 — 2026-10-30
- At the guild trainer, recipes your skill is too low for say "Learn from skill N" in red ([#77](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/77)).

## 0.17.0 — 2026-10-30
- The town is an Italian Renaissance harbor, not a Greek island: Blender-made buildings with arches on columns, terracotta roofs, green shutters and warm plaster ([#76](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/76)).

## 0.16.1 — 2026-10-30
- Every person stands on a Blender-made body with the "Expressive" face: eyes, brows, nose, smile and little shoes ([#75](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/75)). Its own deploy failed and it went live with 0.17.0.

## 0.16.0 — 2026-10-30
- The long game: later ranks are slower (Journeyman crafts x3, Expert x10, Master x20), so long batches matter ([#74](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/74)).
- Expert and Master bottegas burn Lamp Oil and pay their hands a daily wage.
- Recipes and bottega upgrades from Journeyman on cost more, so gold has things to go to.
- The M2 playtest guide ([docs/playtest-m2.md](docs/playtest-m2.md)).

## 0.15.0 — 2026-10-30
- Work your station by hand: an optional short minigame at each station (Work the seam, Harvest by hand, Set the snares) pays extra raw materials. A round tires you, so a few is plenty ([#73](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/73)).

## 0.14.1 — 2026-10-29
- Fix: a Palazzo stall's buy screen stays open in 3D ([#72](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/72)).

## 0.14.0 — 2026-10-29
- First steps: a new-player guide of six steps (collect, craft, train to skill 6, learn a recipe, visit the Palazzo, slap), with ducats for each and a purse at the end ([#70](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/70)).

## 0.13.0 — 2026-10-29
- One square: your quay keeps only your own work, the guild quarters are gone, and the Palazzo is where everyone meets, with a marketplace of guild stalls and the Factotum ([#69](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/69)).

## 0.12.0 — 2026-10-29
- The recipe ladder to 100: 36 new trainer recipes (16 per guild in all), new raw materials, rare recipes and 42 new icons; orders grow with your rank ([#68](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/68)).

## 0.11.0 — 2026-10-29
- The Palazzo: a shared square with the city hall, the Hall of honor, the Wanted board and three officials with daily favors ([#66](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/66), fix [#67](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/67)).
- Your bottega starts as a rundown shack you rebuild, one upgrade per rank, up to the fine bottega.

## 0.10.3 — 2026-10-29
- Behind the scenes: each guild is described once in the game data, so new guilds can be added as content. Nothing changes for players ([#65](https://github.com/MarcialPimentel/guildie/pull/65)).

## 0.10.2 — 2026-10-29

## 0.10.1 — 2026-10-29

## 0.10.0 — 2026-10-29
